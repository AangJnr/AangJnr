## Jambl Beats App

A dynamic music creation app that empowers users to compose beats and melodies effortlessly through its intuitive interface and advanced audio tools. As a Senior Android Engineer, I contributed significantly to the app's development, ensuring performance, stability, and feature parity across platforms.

🔗 [jambl.app](https://jambl.app)

**Tech stack:** Kotlin · C++ · Android · iOS · Mobile App Development
