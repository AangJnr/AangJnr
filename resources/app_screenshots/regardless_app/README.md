## Regardless – Sports & Fitness Platform

A modern mobile platform that connects individuals with sports events, fitness communities, and professional service providers such as gyms and personal trainers.

**Goals:**
1. Enable users to discover and join sports events and communities nearby
2. Provide a streamlined way for service providers to list offerings, manage bookings, and reach a wider audience
3. Encourage participation in healthy, active lifestyles

🔗 [app.regardless.com](https://app.regardless.com)

**Tech stack:** Android · iOS · Spring Boot · Firebase · AI Implementation
